# Li's Vocabulary

A personal, hand-curated collection of **English ↔ Chinese** vocabulary and example
sentences. It is used daily for sentence mining and spaced-repetition review.

## What's inside

- **Basic** cards (word on the front, definition + example on the back)
- **Cloze-deletion** cards for full-sentence recall
- Tagged by topic so you can filter what you study
- Audio and images embedded directly in the package

## How to use this deck

1. **Download** the package from the Baidu Netdisk link below.
2. **Open** it in your browser — no account, no upload, nothing leaves your device.
3. **Review** with the built-in scheduler, or just browse the cards.

> Everything runs locally in your browser. Your review progress is saved on *this*
> device only, so you can close the tab and pick up where you left off.
