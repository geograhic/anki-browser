# Sample Deck

This is a tiny demonstration deck bundled with the site. Use it to try the reviewer
instantly — no download required.

## What's inside

- A couple of **Basic** cards (front/back)
- One **Cloze** card (fill-in-the-blank)
- An embedded image, to show that media renders inline

## Try it

Click **Open in reviewer** on this page. The deck loads straight from the site, and
you can answer the cards with the four rating buttons just like a real Anki session.

> Progress on the sample deck is also saved locally on your device.
